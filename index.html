Right-click a spot on any online map and copy the coordinates. Without them the stop still works, but won't appear on the map or in travel times.

A hike, train, flight or boat can run point-to-point: the main location is where it departs (trailhead, station, airport), this is where it arrives. The commute to the departure point is computed like any other leg, the duration is the leg itself, and the day continues from here. Leave blank when only one point matters (a loop walk, the flight in on arrival day).

Take this trip offline

One HTML file holding the whole itinerary — every day, time, travel leg, note, photo and coordinate, plus a route sketch per day. It loads nothing from the internet, so it opens on a phone in a plane, a tunnel, or a country where the data is switched off.

Keep it on the phone: iPhone → open once in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen (or leave it in Files). Android → keep it in Downloads and open with Chrome. It can be messaged or airdropped to whoever is travelling with you — it needs nothing else to work.
